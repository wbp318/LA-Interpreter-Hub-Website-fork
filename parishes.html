How it works

From parish to draft

Text version of this chart
  1. Pick a parish and check its status flag.
  2. Add local facts and numbers.
  3. Generate the draft.
  4. If any [TO VERIFY] items remain, confirm them with CODOFIL and the district, then regenerate.
  5. When none remain, share the draft with the district and partners.

What the status flags mean. I could not get an authoritative school-by-parish list: CODOFIL’s map did not expose its data. So only 8 parishes carry a status: Confirmed where I read a source, Reported where a search summary said so. The other 56 show Unknown, which means “not confirmed,” not “has no program.” CODOFIL says programs exist in 14 parishes, so some Unknown parishes do have one.
